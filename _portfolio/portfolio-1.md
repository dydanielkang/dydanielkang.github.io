Classical composition performed at 2024 Fermata Spring Concert. The concert painted the life of Echo, and this piece represented her heartbreak from being rejected by Narcissus. There is the grief theme that begins the piece, which transforms throughout the piece. The Nymph theme enters in the middle, and the two themes mix and changes instruments as the music comes to an end.

The audio attached here is a MIDI generated file. There was unfortunately no good audio recording of the concert.
